Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This website and its services are run by Arpit Pandey, a handpan teacher and performer based in Bengaluru, India. This page explains what information is collected when you book a class or workshop, pay a fee, or message us, and how it is used.

What we collect

When you book a workshop, enrol in classes, or make a payment, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, and the details of your booking or payment. Payments are processed by Razorpay; we do not see or store your card, UPI, or bank credentials.

WhatsApp messages

If you opt in to class-related messages, we send you payment links, class reminders, and booking confirmations on WhatsApp through WhatsApp's official Business Platform. If you reply to those messages, your reply is used only to respond to you. You can opt out any time by telling us on WhatsApp or by contacting us below.

How your information is used

Your details are used only to run classes and workshops: scheduling, sending class links and reminders, processing payments, and issuing invoices. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate (payment processing by Razorpay, video classes by Zoom, email by Resend, WhatsApp messaging by Meta).

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements. If you want your contact details removed, write to us and we will remove what we are not legally required to keep.

Contact

For any questions about your data, or to request access or deletion, contact Arpit Pandey at arpithandpan@gmail.com.
