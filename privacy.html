Privacy policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

This website and its services are run by Arpit Pandey, a handpan teacher and performer based in Bengaluru, India. This page explains what information is collected when you book a class or workshop, send an enquiry, pay a fee, or message us, and how it is used.

What we collect

When you book a trial or workshop, enrol in classes, send an enquiry, join the workshop notify list, or make a payment, we collect the details you provide: your name, phone number, email address, and the details of your booking, enquiry or payment. Payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank credentials.

Enquiries and messages

Messages sent through the contact and performance enquiry forms are stored securely and used only to reply to you and arrange the event or class you asked about.

WhatsApp messages

If you opt in to class-related messages, we send you payment links, class reminders and booking confirmations on WhatsApp through WhatsApp’s official Business Platform. If you reply, your reply is used only to respond to you. You can opt out any time by telling us on WhatsApp or by contacting us below.

Analytics and cookies

This website uses the Meta Pixel to understand which pages and adverts lead people to book a trial, a workshop or a performance. It may set cookies in your browser. We also keep small preferences on your device, such as your chosen currency and, if you log in, your student login. You can clear these at any time from your browser settings.

How your information is used

Your details are used only to run classes, workshops and performances: scheduling, sending class links and reminders, replying to enquiries, processing payments and issuing invoices. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing. Information is shared with service providers only as needed to operate: Razorpay for payments, Zoom for video classes, Resend for email, Meta for WhatsApp messaging and the Pixel, and Supabase and Railway for secure data storage.

How long we keep it

Booking and payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal requirements. If you want your contact details removed, write to us and we will remove what we are not legally required to keep.

Contact

For any questions about your data, or to request access or deletion, contact Arpit Pandey at arpithandpan@gmail.com.
