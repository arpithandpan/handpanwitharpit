About Arpit

A video in 2015, and a decade of playing

Arpit grew up in Uttar Pradesh, drawn to rhythm long before he found the handpan. A YouTube video in 2015 stopped him mid-scroll. He got a handpan soon after and hasn’t put it down since.

Today he performs, records and composes from Bengaluru, and has taught through workshops and online classes since 2022. His approach is simple: slow down, listen deeply, and let the playing come from feeling rather than repetition.
