Arpit Pandey is a handpan artist and educator based in India, drawn to the instrument for its unique ability to hold both melody and meditation in the same breath.

He has performed at retreats, cultural festivals, and intimate gatherings across India, and teaches students worldwide through live workshops and online classes.
