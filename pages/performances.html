The sound

Music that changes the air in a room

Arpit’s music sits between the meditative and the melodic. Original compositions, live improvisation that listens to the space, and pieces from his recorded releases.

Every set is shaped by the room and the people in it, whether that is background music while guests arrive, a focused concert, or a quiet session before meditation.

How booking works

From enquiry to the stage

Fees depend on the event, the city, travel and how long you’d like Arpit to play, so every booking gets its own quote.

  1. Send an enquiry

    Tell Arpit about the date, place and feel of your event. Rough details are fine.

  2. Get a reply and a quote

    Arpit reads every enquiry himself and replies by email or WhatsApp with availability and a quote.

  3. Confirm with an advance

    You get a private booking link with the event details and fee. Pay the advance online to lock the date.

  4. Enjoy the music

    Arpit plans the set around your space. The balance is paid through the same link.
