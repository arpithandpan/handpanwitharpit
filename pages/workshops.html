Good to know

Workshop questions

Do I need any experience?
None at all. Workshops are made for complete beginners.
Do I need my own handpan?
No, a handpan is provided for every participant. If you have your own and want to bring it, choose that option when you book.
What if I can’t make it?
Tickets are non-refundable, but you’re welcome to send a friend in your place. Just message Arpit their name before the workshop. If Arpit cancels a workshop, you get a full refund.
Can a friend come and watch?
When a session has audience passes, you’ll see the option while booking, after adding at least one participant ticket.
Anything else to know?
Please handle the instruments with care, since any damage is charged. Photos and short videos are taken during sessions and may be used on social media and to promote future workshops, as set out in the booking terms.
