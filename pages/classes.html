Online classes

Learn handpan from anywhere — live on Zoom, guided by Arpit's patient and thoughtful teaching.

How it works

  1. 1
    Get in touch

    Message Arpit on email, WhatsApp or Instagram to ask about the next batch.

  2. 2
    Join live on Zoom

    Weekly one hour sessions, live. Ask questions and get feedback as you play.

  3. 3
    Practise between sessions

    A week between classes to sit with what you learned. Arpit sends notes on what to work on.

01

Beginner

3 months · 12 sessions · 1hr weekly

Start here if you have never touched a handpan.

First sounds, note layout and basic timing.

Then hand coordination, dynamics and your first piece.

Then flow, improvisation and a final performance.

You finish able to play a short piece confidently and start exploring your own sound.

02

Intermediate

6 months · 24 sessions · 1hr weekly

Start here if you already have a good command of the basics.

Deeper rhythm, polyrhythms and expressive playing.

Common Questions

Do I need to own a handpan to join?
Not necessarily for the very first session, but you'll need one to practise and progress. Arpit can guide you on what to look for if you're planning to buy.
Which level should I start at?
If you've never played handpan before, start with the Beginner course. If you already have some experience and a good grasp of the basics, you can join the Intermediate course. When in doubt, just reach out and Arpit will help you decide.
What do I need for the online class?
A handpan or tongue drum, a stable internet connection, and Zoom installed on your device. That's it.
Are the sessions recorded?
No. Classes are live, so you get feedback in the moment as you play. Arpit will happily go over anything you want to revisit at the start of the next session.
How do I enroll?
Just get in touch via email, WhatsApp, or Instagram. Arpit will share details about the next available batch and answer any questions you have.
Can I get a refund?
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of enrollment, reach out and Arpit will work something out with you. His priority is that you feel the course was worth it.
What payment methods are accepted?
UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, and international cards. Payment is processed securely via Razorpay.
