Is it for you?

Who these lessons are for

A good fit if you are

  • New to the handpan and want a calm, clear start
  • Comfortable with the basics and ready for rhythm, theory and your own pieces
  • Looking for steady weekly practice with someone listening
  • 12 or older. A parent joins the first class for younger students

What you need

  • Your own handpan at home. Not sure which to buy? Ask Arpit on WhatsApp
  • A quiet corner and a stable internet connection
  • Zoom on a laptop, tablet or phone, placed so your hands are visible
  • No music background needed

Inside a class

What happens in your hour

Classes are live, so you get feedback as you play. They are not recorded, and Arpit is happy to go over anything again at the start of the next class.

  1. 01
    Warm upSettle in, wake up the hands and the ears.
  2. 02
    Revision and questionsPlay what you practised and clear up anything that felt stuck.
  3. 03
    New techniques or rhythmsOne new idea at a time, at your pace.
  4. 04
    Play togetherPut it into music with Arpit playing alongside you.
  5. 05
    What to practise nextClear, short goals until the next class.

Questions

Common questions

Do I need to own a handpan?
Yes. Classes are hands on, so you’ll need one at home. Don’t have one yet? Message Arpit on WhatsApp and he’ll help you choose. In Bengaluru, a workshop is a great way to try one first.
How long is the free trial, and what happens in it?
The trial is 45 minutes on Zoom. You’ll play your first sounds, talk about what you want to learn, and figure out your level together. Regular classes after that are 60 minutes.
Which level should I start at?
Never played before? Start with Beginner. Comfortable with the basics? Intermediate. Not sure? You’ll figure it out in the free trial.
Which time zone are classes in?
Yours. The trial page shows every time in your local time, and your weekly class stays at the same local time even when clocks change for daylight saving.
What if I need to miss a class?
Move or skip a class yourself from your student page, at least 12 hours before it starts. After skipping, you can book a make-up time later in the same month. Less than 12 hours to go? Message Arpit.
Can children join?
Yes, from age 12. A parent joins the first class so everyone knows how lessons work.
Are classes recorded?
No. Classes are live, so you get feedback in the moment as you play. Arpit will happily go over anything again at the start of the next class.
How do I pay, and can I get a refund?
In India: UPI, cards and net banking. Outside India: any international card. All payments go securely through Razorpay. Fees aren’t refunded, but if something comes up, message Arpit and you’ll work it out.
I already play. Can I skip the trial?
Yes. Request a weekly time directly. Arpit confirms it, then you choose a plan and pay.
