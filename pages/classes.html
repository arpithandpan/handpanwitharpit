Learn Online

Online Classes

Learn handpan from anywhere in the world — at your own pace, guided by Arpit's patient and thoughtful teaching.

These courses are built around a simple belief: the handpan is an instrument of listening first, and technique second. You don't need to be a musician. You just need to be willing to slow down and pay attention.

Each course is a series of video lessons that you can watch and revisit at any time. Arpit teaches directly to camera — unhurried, clear, and always focused on helping you find your own relationship with the instrument.

Students from India, the UK, Germany, Australia, and the US have gone through these courses. Whatever your timezone or background, you're welcome here.

Common Questions

Do I need to own a handpan to enroll?
No — you can begin Level 01 without owning an instrument. The early lessons cover understanding the handpan, its history, and how it works. However, you'll get much more out of the course if you have access to one. Arpit can suggest affordable options if you're looking to buy your first instrument.
What level should I start at?
If you've never touched a handpan, start at Level 01. If you've played a bit but feel unsure of your foundations, Level 01 or 02. If you've been playing for a year or more and feel comfortable with basic melodies, Level 02 or 03. When in doubt, drop Arpit a message and he'll help you decide.
How does feedback work?
In Level 02 and Level 03, you can send video recordings of your practice to Arpit via email or WhatsApp. He'll respond with personalised written or voice note feedback within 3–5 working days. Level 03 also includes a monthly 30-minute video call with Arpit.
Is this a subscription or one-time payment?
One-time payment. Once you enroll, the course is yours forever — including any future updates Arpit adds to the curriculum. No recurring charges.
Can I get a refund?
If you're not satisfied within the first 7 days of enrollment, reach out and Arpit will work something out with you. His priority is that you feel the course was worth it.
What payment methods are accepted?
UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, and international cards. Payment is processed securely via Razorpay.
