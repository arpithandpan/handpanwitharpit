Questions

Common questions

What happens in a class?
A warm up, a look at what you practised, one new technique or rhythm, some playing together with Arpit, and clear goals for the week ahead. Classes are live and not recorded.
How long is the free trial, and what happens in it?
The trial is 45 minutes on Zoom. You’ll play your first sounds, talk about what you want to learn, and figure out your level together. Regular classes after that are 60 minutes.
Which time zone are classes in?
Yours. The trial page shows every time in your local time, and your weekly class stays at the same local time even when clocks change for daylight saving.
What if I need to miss a class?
Move or skip a class yourself from your student page, at least 12 hours before it starts. After skipping, you can book a make-up time later in the same month. Less than 12 hours to go? Message Arpit.
How do I pay, and can I get a refund?
In India: UPI, cards and net banking. Outside India: any international card. All payments go securely through Razorpay. Fees aren’t refunded, but if something comes up, message Arpit and you’ll work it out.
I already play. Can I skip the trial?
Yes. Request a weekly time directly. Arpit confirms it, then you choose a plan and pay.
